Make the Most of Your Stay

🏛️ Keep the adventure going

Staying a little longer? Turn your post-event days into an unforgettable Egyptian escape, filled with history, culture, and exceptional experiences.

🏛️ Wander through Old Cairo & Khan el-Khalili

Step into the story of Islamic Cairo with a fascinating architectural walk, then browse the vibrant lanes of Khan el-Khalili for handcrafted treasures and local atmosphere.

✨ Meet the Royal Mummies at NMEC

Discover Egypt’s extraordinary past at the National Museum of Egyptian Civilization and experience the captivating Royal Mummies exhibition.

🍽️ Indulge in Executive Dining

End your Cairo adventure in style with top-tier dining, from elegant Nile-side restaurants in Zamalek to standout culinary experiences across West Cairo.
