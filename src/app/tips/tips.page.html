FINANCIAL

Currency & Payments

Official Currency EGP (Egyptian Pound)

Major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard) are widely accepted across the venue, hotels, and upscale restaurants.

ATMs & Exchange: Accessible on-site at the hotel lobby and Cairo International Airport.
Cash on Hand: It is advisable to carry some cash in Egyptian pounds for added convenience when visiting Egypt’s historical landmarks, cultural attractions, and traditional destinations.

LOCAL EXPERIENCES

Make the Most of Your Stay in Egypt

Extend your journey with handpicked cultural landmarks, culinary gems, and architectural wonders.

HERITAGE & SHOPPING 2-3 Hours

Wander through Historic Cairo & Khan el-Khalili

Step into 1,000 years of living history. Walk along Al-Mu’izz Street—one of the world's richest concentrations of medieval Islamic architecture—before exploring the historic lanes of Khan el-Khalili for hand-crafted lanterns, aromatic spices, and traditional silversmiths.

Must-Try Sip fresh mint tea at the historic El Fishawy Café (est. 1773).
WORLD-CLASS MUSEUM 2 Hours

The Royal Mummies at NMEC

The National Museum of Egyptian Civilization (NMEC) in Fustat offers a chronological journey through 5,000+ years of civilization. The centerpiece is the subterranean Royal Mummies Hall, featuring 22 ancient pharaohs and queens displayed with state-of-the-art conservation technology.

Highlight Overlooking Ain El Sira Lake with spectacular sunset views.
ICONIC WONDER Half Day

Giza Plateau & The Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM)

Stand in awe before the Great Pyramid of Khufu, the Sphinx, and the architectural triumph of the Grand Egyptian Museum. Experience monumental statues, the grand staircase, and breathtaking panoramic views of the pyramids.

Architecture Book admission tickets online in advance for expedited entry.
CULINARY & LIFESTYLE Evening

Nile-Side Executive Dining & Zamalek

Celebrate the conclusion of the event at Cairo’s premier waterfront venues. Zamalek island hosts world-class contemporary Mediterranean and authentic Middle Eastern dining on docked yachts and open-air riverside terraces.

Dining Reservations recommended for peak evening terrace tables.
